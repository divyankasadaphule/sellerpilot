
# SellerPilot | Primary Research

## 1. Research Overview

**Project:** SellerPilot  
**Research phase:** Milestone 2 – Product Discovery  
**Method:** Exploratory qualitative research using asynchronous questionnaires  
**Participants:** 2 small ecommerce business owners  
**Platform used by both participants:** Shopify  
**Research status:** Completed for initial concept development

### Research Objective

To understand how small ecommerce business owners manage order fulfillment, shipping, returns, customer communication, and operational challenges.

The research explores whether shipping exceptions and logistics coordination represent meaningful problems for potential SellerPilot users.

### Research Questions

1. How do sellers manage orders from confirmation to delivery?
2. What operational activities are most challenging?
3. How do sellers identify and resolve shipping problems?
4. How are returns, refunds, and exchanges handled?
5. How do sellers communicate with customers?
6. Which tools support their daily operations?
7. What problems require additional effort to resolve?
8. What improvements would sellers prioritize?

---

## 2. Research Methodology

Two small ecommerce business owners responded to eight open-ended questions.

- P01 provided written responses through WhatsApp.
- P02 provided voice-message responses, which were transcribed for analysis.

Participants were recruited through personal contacts.

The interviews were exploratory rather than statistically representative.

### Limitations

- Small sample size of two participants.
- Participants were recruited through convenience sampling.
- Both participants use Shopify.
- Order volumes and detailed business metrics were not collected.
- Responses were self-reported rather than directly observed.
- P02's responses were analyzed from a supplied transcription.
- Findings cannot establish the prevalence of shipping problems among small ecommerce businesses.

The results will inform an initial design concept rather than establish market-wide validation.

## 3. Participant P01

**Participant ID:** P01  
**Business:** Noble Misfit  
**Category:** Fashion  
**Business started:** March 2026  
**Platform:** Shopify  
**Research method:** Written WhatsApp questionnaire

### Interview Evidence

**Q1. Order fulfillment process**

The participant manually verifies order details, prepares packaging, creates shipping through Shopify, and dispatches the order.

**Q2. Most challenging operations**

Managing the complete business lifecycle requires attention to product development, marketing, lead generation, and fulfillment.

The participant identified lead generation and driving sales as the greatest challenge.

**Q3. Shipping problems**

No shipping or missing-delivery issues were reported.

**Q4. Returns and refunds**

The participant described using Shopify's built-in return functionality.

**Q5. Customer communication**

Customers contact the business through email or the website contact page.

**Q6. Additional tools**

No additional operational tools were reported.

**Q7. Difficult customer issues**

No significant customer issues were reported.

The participant described Shopify's processes as seamless.

**Q8. Desired improvement**

Lead generation and customer acquisition.

### P01 Summary

P01 did not report significant shipping or fulfillment problems.

Her primary challenge relates to generating demand and attracting customers.

This finding challenges the assumption that all small ecommerce sellers experience substantial shipping-management difficulties.

## 4. Participant P02

**Participant ID:** P02  
**Business category:** Pooja samagri / religious and worship products  
**Business started:** August 11, 2025  
**Platform:** Shopify  
**Research method:** WhatsApp voice messages, transcribed

### Interview Evidence

**Q1. Order fulfillment process**

The seller retrieves ordered products, packs them, measures the packages, and enters shipment information into logistics software.

The logistics provider then arranges pickup and delivery.

**Q2. Most challenging operations**

The participant identified logistics as the most challenging operational area.

Reported difficulties included:

- Missed pickups
- Delivery delays
- Late returns
- Lost shipments
- Repeated coordination with logistics providers

Additional challenges included advertising performance and business accounting.

**Q3. Shipping problems**

The participant reported delivery delays and incorrect shipments.

She described an experience involving Amazon Shipping in India, where a customer received an unrelated product.

**Q4. Returns and refunds**

Returns are relatively uncommon because the business uses prepaid or partially prepaid orders.

Problems may occur when customers are unavailable to receive shipments after multiple delivery attempts.

The seller contacts customers and coordinates the return of undelivered parcels.

**Q5. Customer communication**

Customers contact the business through email and the website's contact functionality.

The seller checks incoming messages and inquiries.

**Q6. Additional tools**

The business uses:

- Shopify for ecommerce operations
- An integrated logistics partner for shipping
- Excel for inventory management

Inventory is checked approximately every two weeks or monthly.

**Q7. Difficult customer issue**

A customer who ordered a bell received an unexpectedly large, incorrect parcel.

The seller attributed the error to Amazon Shipping.

The business had to:

1. Respond to the customer's complaint.
2. Apologize for the incorrect delivery.
3. Send the correct product.
4. Pursue a claim or refund with the logistics provider.

This example illustrates how shipping errors can create additional customer service and coordination work.

**Q8. Desired improvement**

The participant wanted better logistics visibility, particularly notifications about delayed shipments and missed pickups.

She described having to continually monitor shipment locations and statuses.

### Selected Participant Statements

> "Logistics is the most painful part."

> "We constantly have to keep track of where the products are."

> "These many orders are delayed, this is what is happening."

These excerpts are from the user-provided transcription and should be checked against the original audio before publication as verbatim quotations.

### P02 Summary

P02 reported recurring logistics difficulties involving external shipping providers.

The participant expressed interest in improved visibility into delayed shipments, pickup issues, and outstanding delivery problems.

This provides initial evidence supporting further investigation of shipping exception management.

## 5. Preliminary Comparison

|---|---|---|
| Business category | Fashion | Pooja samagri |
| Started | March 2026 | August 2025 |
| Ecommerce platform | Shopify | Shopify |
| Shipping issues | None reported | Multiple reported |
| Operational tools | Shopify | Shopify, Excel, logistics software |
| Primary challenge | Lead generation | Logistics |
| Desired improvement | Customer acquisition | Shipping visibility and coordination |

### Interpretation

The participants reported contrasting experiences.

P01 described relatively smooth fulfillment, while P02 experienced recurring logistics difficulties.

Potential contributing factors include business maturity, order volume, product category, shipping providers, and operational arrangements.

These explanations remain unverified.

## 6. Preliminary Research Findings

**F01 – Operational challenges vary between sellers.**

The two participants reported different primary difficulties.

**F02 – Shipping exceptions are a meaningful problem for at least one participant.**

P02 described delays, missed pickups, lost shipments, and incorrect deliveries.

**F03 – Shipping problems can create additional coordination work.**

P02's incorrect-delivery example involved customer communication, replacement fulfillment, and a logistics claim.

**F04 – Sellers may use multiple operational systems.**

P02 reported using Shopify, logistics software, and Excel.

**F05 – Better shipment visibility is a potential design opportunity.**

P02 specifically requested clearer information about delays and pickups.

**F06 – Existing ecommerce tools may sufficiently support some sellers.**

P01 reported no significant shipping issues and described Shopify's processes as seamless.

## 7. Implications for SellerPilot

### Initial Design Direction

Explore an AI-assisted shipping exception management experience for small ecommerce sellers who experience recurring logistics difficulties.

Potential capabilities to investigate:

- Identifying shipments requiring attention
- Reviewing shipment and exception details
- Understanding available evidence
- Suggesting appropriate next steps
- Supporting customer communication
- Tracking issues through resolution

These are design hypotheses, not validated product requirements.

### Provisional Target User

Small ecommerce business owners or operators who personally coordinate fulfillment and experience recurring shipping exceptions involving logistics providers.

### Provisional Problem Statement

Small ecommerce sellers managing fulfillment through ecommerce and logistics systems may struggle to identify, investigate, and resolve shipping exceptions efficiently.

These difficulties can require repeated monitoring, follow-ups, and coordination alongside other business responsibilities.

### Scope Decision

The initial SellerPilot prototype will focus on shipping exception visibility and coordination.

Returns, marketing, accounting, and broader ecommerce operations remain outside the initial MVP scope.

AI functionality will be explored as decision support, with sellers retaining control over consequential actions.

## 8. Next Steps

2. Create a service blueprint covering sellers, ecommerce systems, and logistics providers.
3. Define information architecture and key user flows.
4. Develop low-fidelity wireframes and interactive prototypes.
5. Conduct usability testing with two business owners and three non-business owners.
6. Document findings, limitations, and design iterations.

The planned usability testing will assess the prototype's usability and selected workflow assumptions. It will not establish broad market demand.

---

## 9. Research Ethics and Documentation

Participant responses should be treated as confidential research material.

Public-facing case studies should use participant IDs or obtain explicit permission before identifying individuals or businesses.

Any verbatim quotations should be checked against the original responses.

Interpretations, assumptions, and proposed solutions must remain distinguishable from participant evidence.
