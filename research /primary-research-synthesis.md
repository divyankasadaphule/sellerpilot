
# SellerPilot | Primary Research Synthesis

**Milestone:** 2 – Product Discovery  
**Participants:** 2 small ecommerce business owners  
**Research method:** Asynchronous qualitative questionnaires  
**Status:** Initial synthesis completed

## 1. Research Objective

Understand the operational challenges experienced by small online sellers and identify potential opportunities for improving shipping exception management.

## 2. Key Research Findings

### Finding 1: Operational challenges differ between sellers

P01 identified lead generation as her primary challenge and reported no significant shipping problems.

P02 identified logistics as her most difficult operational responsibility, describing missed pickups, delayed deliveries, and incorrect shipments.

**Interpretation:** Not all small ecommerce sellers experience the same operational problems.

### Finding 2: Shipping exceptions can create additional coordination work

P02 described an incident in which a customer received an incorrect parcel.

Resolving the issue required customer communication, replacement fulfillment, and a claim with the logistics provider.

**Interpretation:** Shipping errors can create work beyond the original fulfillment process.

### Finding 3: Some sellers manage operations across multiple tools

P02 uses Shopify, integrated logistics software, and Excel for inventory management.

P01 reported using Shopify without additional operational tools.

**Interpretation:** Operational complexity and tool usage differ between participants. The research does not establish that switching between tools is itself a significant pain point.

### Finding 4: Proactive shipping visibility may be valuable

P02 expressed interest in being notified about delayed shipments and missed pickups.

**Interpretation:** Timely exception information is a potential design opportunity.

We have not yet established whether existing logistics tools already provide these capabilities or why they may be insufficient.

### Finding 5: Existing tools may already meet some sellers' needs

P01 described Shopify's order management process as seamless.

**Interpretation:** SellerPilot should not assume that all Shopify sellers require additional operational software.

## 3. Evidence and Design Opportunities

| Evidence | Potential Opportunity | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| P02 reported recurring shipping issues | Shipping exception overview | Exploratory |
| P02 requested timely notifications | Exception alerts and prioritization | Exploratory |
| P02 described an incorrect delivery | Structured issue investigation | Exploratory |
| P02 uses several operational tools | Consolidated shipment context | Hypothesis |
| P01 reported smooth fulfillment | Focus on sellers with recurring exceptions | Directional |

Confidence labels reflect the limited evidence available and are not statistical measures.

## 4. Contrasting Findings

P01 and P02 reported different experiences.

P01:
- Fashion business established March 2026
- No significant shipping issues reported
- Lead generation identified as the biggest challenge

P02:
- Pooja samagri business established August 11, 2025
- Recurring logistics problems reported
- Shipping visibility and coordination identified as desired improvements

Possible explanations include order volume, business maturity, product characteristics, shipping arrangements, and delivery geography.

These factors were not sufficiently investigated to establish why the participants' experiences differ.

## 5. Refined Target Audience

### Initial Audience

Small ecommerce business owners managing online operations.

### Provisional Audience After Research

Small ecommerce sellers who personally coordinate fulfillment and experience recurring shipping exceptions involving logistics providers.

This segment is a design hypothesis, not a validated market segment.

## 6. Refined Problem Statement

Small ecommerce sellers who experience recurring shipping exceptions may struggle to identify, investigate, and coordinate the resolution of delayed pickups, delivery errors, and other shipment problems.

These activities can require repeated monitoring and follow-ups alongside other business responsibilities.

## 7. Product Direction

SellerPilot will explore an AI-assisted shipping exception management workspace.

The initial concept will focus on:

1. Identifying shipments requiring attention.
2. Reviewing shipment details and exception history.
3. Understanding possible next steps.
4. Supporting seller-controlled communication and action.
5. Tracking issues through resolution.

These are proposed capabilities, not validated requirements.

AI assistance will be explored for summarization and decision support. The research has not yet demonstrated that AI is necessary.

## 8. Research Limitations

- Two participants recruited through personal contacts
- Both participants use Shopify
- Self-reported rather than observational research
- Unknown order volumes
- Limited understanding of existing shipping notification features
- No evidence establishing how widespread the reported problems are
- No direct testing of the proposed SellerPilot solution

## 9. Design Decision

Proceed with shipping exception management as the initial design exploration.

This decision is based on:
- P02's reported logistics difficulties
- Contrasting evidence from P01
- Existing secondary research
- The project's objective of exploring complex B2B and AI-assisted workflows

The decision does not imply broad problem or market validation.

## 10. Next Steps
