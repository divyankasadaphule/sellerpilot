
# SellerPilot: Research Objectives & Assumptions

## Research Approach
This project uses publicly available secondary research, including ecommerce seller discussions, competitor documentation, and industry resources. No primary interviews have been conducted.

## Research Goal
Understand the challenges small ecommerce sellers may face when resolving post-purchase customer issues, particularly missing-delivery complaints.

## Research Objectives
1. Investigate post-purchase problems reported by ecommerce sellers.
2. Understand how delivery disputes are currently resolved.
3. Examine existing ecommerce customer support tools.
4. Identify potential opportunities to improve issue resolution.
5. Explore AI assistance with human oversight.

## Initial Assumptions (Unvalidated)
- Sellers may switch between multiple tools to resolve issues.
- Conflicting tracking information may make decisions difficult.
- A unified view of order information may be useful.
- AI recommendations may be more useful when accompanied by explanations.
- Sellers may prefer to approve refunds and other consequential actions.

## Research Limitations
Public discussions are anecdotal and may not represent all ecommerce sellers. Competitor documentation does not establish usability. Findings will be separated from assumptions and design hypotheses.

## Next Steps
Collect secondary evidence, analyze competitors, map the service journey, and synthesize findings.
